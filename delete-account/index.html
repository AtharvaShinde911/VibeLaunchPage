Delete Account

Last Updated: 1 September 2026

If you use the Vibzee mobile app and would like to delete your account, you can submit a deletion request using the form below. Submitting this form only records your request — it does not delete your account immediately.

How to Request Account Deletion

Fill out the form below to submit a deletion request. Your details will be saved for our team to review. Account deletion is processed manually after verification. You can also email support@spartacantech.com if you prefer.

What Happens After Your Request

Your request is stored securely and marked for review. Our team will verify the details and process account deletion separately. Following a verified deletion request, the user's photograph stored in Vibzee's backend/cloud storage will be deleted within 30 days, subject to any information that Vibzee is legally required or permitted to retain.

Contact

For questions about account deletion, contact us at support@spartacantech.com.
